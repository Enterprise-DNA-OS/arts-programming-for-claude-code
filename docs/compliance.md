# Programming record checks

Sources checked 16 November 2026. These checks find evidence gaps. They neither grant performance rights nor decide that an event is safe. The producer retains responsibility for applicable permissions and duties.

[APRA AMCOS theatrical licensing](https://www.apraamcos.co.nz/music-licences/select-a-licence/theatrical) says dramatic-context performances need approval from the relevant rights owners and that some works must be licensed directly. It asks applicants to apply at least six weeks before the first public performance. This is application guidance, not an automatic statutory deadline. Record each licence's actual dates, scope and evidence; this tool never computes a royalty or sends an application.

[WorkSafe: managing risks at events](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/event-management/managing-risks-at-events/) describes identifying hazards, applying and reviewing controls, worker involvement and coordination with other businesses. The controls here record that review; they cannot judge its adequacy. For Australian events obtain the applicable state or territory requirements and add local checks. The New Zealand guidance is not presented as Australian law.

| Rule | What is checked | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| R01 | Each active performance has current programme clearance, recorded territory/context and date coverage | Local evidence check supporting rights-owner approval. Does not check every right, rights share or licence condition |
| R02 | A safety review and consultation reference exist for the current call revision | Local implementation supporting WorkSafe review and coordination guidance |
| R03 | Planned people do not exceed the operator-entered capacity | Local capacity exception, not a determination of lawful venue capacity |
| R04 | Calls fall inside the recorded season dates in the production timezone | Local scheduling rule |
| R05 | Production review date has not passed | Operator-set review cadence |
| R06 | Open action has not passed its due date | Operator-set follow-up |
| R07 | An open production has programme items | Local record completeness |
| R08 | Programme credit wording is present | Local drafting check, not verification of a copyright credit obligation |
| R09 | Active calls do not overlap or consume a space's turnaround allowance | Local booking rule. Back-to-back calls at exactly the required allowance pass |

An expired, revoked or superseded clearance remains visible. A programme revision invalidates earlier clearance evidence. A changed call needs a new safety review. Approval and exemption require authority and evidence, but this is a trusted-operator database, not an identity-verification or electronic-signature system. All timestamps display in UTC; rights and season date checks use the production's explicit timezone.
